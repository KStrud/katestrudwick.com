Frida Kahlo

Angled exhibition graphics for Making Her Self Up at the V&A.

Back
BOB Design, 2018

Using materials, typography and angles to show Frida Kahlo’s duality: a strong, powerful woman whose body was tormented by injury.

Kahlo used clothing to express herself and as armour. A childhood accident left her with lifelong health problems, and she spent long periods bedridden, painting on an angled canvas with a mirror above her.

I developed the initial concept of angled signage with hidden reflections beneath the surface, then prototyped it for manufacture.

The angular typography and signage honour her strength during her hardest times. Captions were lined with metallic material, hinting at both her inner strength and beauty and her physical fragility.
