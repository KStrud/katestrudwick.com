Google Playspace Labs, 2024

Working side by side with an AI agent to find out how it should behave as a teammate.

Playspace Labs was one of the first teams at Google to work on AI agents, before most people were talking about them. There were no patterns to follow, so we had to invent new ones.

We prototyped by working side by side with an agent ourselves, treating it like any other member of the team, to uncover how it should function. Should it feel like a human colleague, or clearly like an agent? When should it step in, and how should it check in with people?

I designed the use cases, the screens, the user flows and the conversations. The result was AI Teammate, a Gemini-powered agent with its own Workspace account, presented on stage in the Google I/O 2024 keynote.
