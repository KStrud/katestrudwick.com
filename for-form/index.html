Rethinking how detectives package and track forensic evidence.

Back
Imperial College × RCA, 2020

Bringing design thinking and material innovation to detectives, to redefine how forensic evidence is packaged and processed.

The way evidence is collected, packaged and processed is outdated and unreliable, which raises the risk of contamination. In many cases only five pieces of evidence make it to court, so each one needs the best possible chance of surviving trial.

I spent time on site with detectives in police stations, evidence lockers and courtrooms. With advice from forensic scientists, I developed a new packaging material, then used it with a set of props to co-design the rest of the system with the detectives themselves.

The result is a flexible, mouldable material that both protects and packages evidence; a traceable, secure seal; and an embedded RFID chip that logs every step of the chain of custody automatically.
