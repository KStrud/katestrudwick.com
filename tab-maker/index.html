Tab Maker

A no-code tool for building Chrome new-tab extensions from a Google Sheet.

Back
Google Creative Lab, 2021

Bringing Google Sheets and a no-code interface to everyone, so they can take over the new tab page.

Getting work in front of people online is hard. Chrome’s new tab page is seen constantly, but customising it takes code, which shuts most people out.

It began as my own extension, showing work by creatives of colour on every new tab, fed by a Google Sheet. I designed the UX and UI for a no-code version, built it with developers, and creatively directed the website, film and social assets.

Tab Maker lets anyone design a new tab extension, using a Google Sheet as the content feed so creators can update and collaborate. It launched as a Google Experiment; MoMA used it for their own extension, and it took off with educators.
