Fountains of Hygiene, 2020

A paintable, colour-changing hand sanitiser that makes clean hands fun for children.

Hand sanitiser use soared during the pandemic. In a one-day sprint, we asked how to make it as fun and engaging as possible for kids.

I came up with the concept and created the poster to explain it.

The gel contains natural pH-responsive pigments that change colour as you rub it into your hands, and the bottle is designed as a paintbrush so children can apply it themselves.
