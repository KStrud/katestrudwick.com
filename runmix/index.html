Running playlists built around the tempo you want to run at. Your speed is the DJ.

RunMix lets you make playlists based on the pace you want to keep. Simply choose a genre and hit the track.

As you run, your speed acts like a DJ on a deck, speeding the music up or slowing it down. To keep the beat sounding right, you have to run at the right pace.

The interface turns pace into something you can see and hear, making keeping the pace feel intuitive and enjoyable.
