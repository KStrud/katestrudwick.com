Cardiac Stimulator

Simplifying how surgeons control cardiac ablation equipment.

Back
Imperial College Hospital, 2019

Applying user research and UX design with cardiologists to simplify how surgeons control cardiac ablation equipment.

Existing cardiology devices were so complex that surgeons used standard computer keyboards as consoles, with functions that were hard to learn and easy to trigger by accident.

I ran user research with key hospital stakeholders, mapped the device’s core functions, built CAD models of a new console and directed the design of its interface, working on site to observe and co-design with doctors.

The result combined two separate consoles into one multi-functional device with a matching GUI. The designs were used to secure funding to build it.
